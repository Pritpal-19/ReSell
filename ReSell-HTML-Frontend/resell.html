Help & Support

Find answers to the most commonly asked questions here.

How do I post a free ad?

Posting an ad is easy! Just click on the + SELL button on the home page, login to your account, fill in your product details, upload photos, and click post.

How do I contact a seller?

Open the product you like, and click on the Contact Seller button. You will see options to Call, WhatsApp, or Email the seller directly.

Is my data secure?

Yes. ReSell stores your data securely using MongoDB and encrypted connections. Only verified sellers can post products.

Legal & Privacy Information

1. Privacy Policy

We value your privacy. We collect basic information like email and phone numbers strictly for the purpose of connecting buyers and sellers. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.


2. Terms of Service

By using ReSell, you agree to post only genuine, legal, and authentic items. Fraudulent listings, illegal goods, or spam will result in a permanent ban from the platform.


3. Buyer Protection

We advise all buyers to physically inspect the items before making any payments. ReSell is not responsible for transactions that happen outside the platform.
